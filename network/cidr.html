The number after the slash is a bit count, nothing more. It says: "starting from the left, this many bits of the mask are locked to 1 — the rest are 0." /24 means 24 ones then 8 zeros, which written in dotted decimal is 255.255.255.0. There's no separate concept to learn — the mask and the slash number are two spellings of the same fact.
